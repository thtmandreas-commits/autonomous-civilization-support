PRIVACY POLICY // EFFECTIVE 28 JULY 2026

Private by design.

Autonomous Civilization is a single-player macOS game designed to work entirely on your Mac.

Data collection

Autonomous Civilization does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not use:

  • user accounts
  • advertising or analytics
  • tracking technologies
  • third-party software development kits
  • cloud storage or online multiplayer
  • user-generated content

Local game data

Campaign saves and preferences are stored locally inside the app’s macOS sandbox. This may include your selected bloc, campaign progress, decisions, settings, and deterministic campaign seed.

Local game data remains on your Mac unless you independently copy, back up, or transfer files using operating-system features. Individual manual saves can be deleted through the in-app save manager.

Network access

Autonomous Civilization does not require an internet connection and does not send gameplay or personal data to the developer.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users because it does not collect personal information.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds online services or changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before those changes are released.

For privacy questions, use the contact method on the support page.
